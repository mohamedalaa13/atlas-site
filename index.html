Atlas is a workspace for companies that still run on paper, spreadsheets and group chats. Assign the day's work, approve leave with the balance already calculated, and keep employee records somewhere they can actually be found.

What Atlas does

Tasks and accountability

Assign work with deadlines, estimates and priorities. Attach a photo as proof a job was done. Filter by status, assignee, site or due date.

Leave and requests

Balances that update themselves when a request is approved. Define your own leave types to match your country's rules. Salary advances, documents, equipment and overtime claims too.

Employee records

Employment details, work and emergency contacts, and licence expiry dates. Payroll and identity records are kept in a separate, restricted store.

Company feed

Announcements everyone sees and nobody can quietly delete. Posts restricted to managers when they need to be.

Reporting

A weekly report per person and per department, built automatically every Sunday. Export everything your company owns as a file, any time.

Built for the field

Works offline and syncs when the signal returns. English and Arabic with full right-to-left support. Sign in by phone number — no password.

Pricing

Minimum 10 people. Billed monthly in USD. Your local sales tax, if any, is added at checkout. One plan — every feature is included.

You pay for the people you cover, not your headcount

You do not have to put the whole company on Atlas. Cover a team, a department, or a set of job titles, and add the rest when it has proved itself. Everyone else can still join the company; they simply wait to be added. Seat changes apply to your next invoice — moving people in and out never triggers a charge.

Refunds and cancellation

Cancelling

Cancel at any time. Your subscription runs to the end of the period you have already paid for, and is not renewed. Your company's data stays available to read for 90 days after that, so nothing is lost while you decide.

Refunds

  • Within 14 days of your first payment — full refund, no questions asked, if Atlas is not right for you.
  • If Atlas is unavailable or materially broken for a sustained period in a paid month, a refund of that month in full or in part.
  • If you were charged in error — duplicate payment, a subscription you had already cancelled — a full refund of the incorrect charge.

Outside those cases we do not refund part-months, because you keep access for the whole period you paid for. Email mohmd46alaa@gmail.com with your company name and we will reply within two working days. Refunds are issued by Paddle to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 working days.
